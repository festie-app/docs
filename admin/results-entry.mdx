---
title: "Results Entry"
description: "Enter positions and grades for programmes manually or via judge scores."
---

Festie supports three types of result entry: **Manual Result**, **Manual Score**, and **Online Judgement**. 

## 1. Manual Result (Fastest)
Use this if your judges have already calculated everything on paper, and you just need to enter who came 1st, 2nd, and 3rd.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Select a programme and ensure its judgement type is set to **Manual Result**.
3. You will see a list of registered candidates.
4. Simply select the Position (e.g., First) and Grade (e.g., A Grade) for the winning candidates.
5. Click **Save Result**.

## 2. Manual Score (Calculated)
Use this if you want the system to calculate the final positions based on raw scores given by multiple judges.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Select a programme and change its judgement type to **Manual Score**.
3. Click **Manage Judges** and add your judges (e.g., Judge A, Judge B, Judge C).
4. Enter the maximum possible score for each judge (e.g., Out of 10 or 20).
5. For each candidate, enter the raw score given by each judge.
6. Click **Save Changes** and then click **Generate Result**.
7. Festie will automatically calculate the totals and assign 1st, 2nd, and 3rd positions!

## Enter a total for an online judge

Use this when a programme uses **Online Judgement** and a judge gave their marks on paper instead of in the judge panel. You type that judge's total for each candidate yourself. Not available for round-based programmes.

1. Go to **Results** and open the programme.
2. Open the **Candidate wise** tab.
3. Click **Edit score**. Only columns of judges who haven't scored online become editable.
4. In that judge's column, type the total for each candidate.
5. Click **Save** (or **Save & generate** to also generate the result).

Saving a total locks that judge.

- **The keyed judge is locked.** They can no longer score this programme in the judge panel.
- **Other judges keep scoring.** Judges you didn't key in can still score as normal.
- **Judges who already scored online are view-only.** You can't type over their scores in this table.
- **The lock stays.** Changing the judgement status (**On**, **Off** or **Schedule**) does not release it.
- **To release the judge,** remove that judge's scores or unlock the judge.

<Note>
  Once results are generated, judges can't score until the results are removed.
</Note>

Totals always come from the judges. You can't type a candidate's final total by hand.

## Revert edited totals

Older versions of Festie let admins type a candidate's final total directly. If a programme still has totals edited that way, its rows show an **Edited** badge. Judge-by-judge editing stays off, and you see "Totals edited. Revert to edit judge-wise."

**Who can do this:** admins whose role has the **Revert edited totals** permission (Programmes, actions).

1. Open the programme and go to the **Scores** tab.
2. Click **Revert to judge scores**.
3. In the confirmation, click **Revert**.

Festie removes the edited totals and regenerates the results from the judge scores. You can then edit judge by judge again.

## Mark a programme to review

Use this to flag a programme that is scored but needs a second look before you generate its results. Flagged programmes get a **To review** badge, and you can filter the Results page to see only them.

**Who can do this:** admins whose role has the **Mark to review** permission ("Flag evaluated programmes to review."). Roles that can already manage results get it automatically. Judges and teams can't.

**When it is available:** the programme is **Evaluated** (scored, results not generated yet). The button doesn't show for other programmes.

### Mark one programme

1. Go to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Open an **Evaluated** programme.
3. In the drawer header, click **Mark to review**.

The **To review** badge appears next to the status badge on the programme card and at the top of the drawer. The **Mark to review** button becomes a filled **Mark as resolved** button. Click it to remove the flag.

The **⋯** (More actions) menu in the header holds other actions: **Judgement** (change the judgement type), **History** and **Score settings**. It does not hold the review buttons.

### Mark several programmes

1. Go to `Subfest > Results` and click **Select**.
2. Tick the programmes.
3. In the bar at the bottom, click **Mark to review** or **Mark as resolved**.

Programmes that are not **Evaluated** are skipped.

### Show only flagged programmes

Use the **To review** filter in the filter row on the Results page, after **Position Allowed**. Choose **Yes** to see only flagged programmes, or **No** to hide them.

This filter only shows on the **All** and **Scored** tabs. It is most useful on the **Scored** tab.

### When the flag clears

- Festie clears it when results are generated for the programme.
- Festie clears it when you remove the scores and the programme goes back to **Draft**.
- Saving scores again does **not** clear it. Click **Mark as resolved** yourself.

<Note>
  The flag belongs to one sub-festival. It is not copied to other sub-festivals when you sync settings.
</Note>

<Note>
  Saving a result does **not** automatically publish it to the public website. You must use the Result Release feature to make it public.
</Note>
