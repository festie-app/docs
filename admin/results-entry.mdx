---
title: "Results Entry"
description: "Enter positions and grades for programmes manually or via judge scores."
---

Festie supports three types of result entry: **Manual Result**, **Manual Score**, and **Online Judgement**. 

## 1. Manual Result (Fastest)
Use this if your judges have already calculated everything on paper, and you just need to enter who came 1st, 2nd, and 3rd.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Select a programme and ensure its judgement type is set to **Manual Result**.
3. You will see a list of registered candidates.
4. Simply select the Position (e.g., First) and Grade (e.g., A Grade) for the winning candidates.
5. Click **Save Result**.

## 2. Manual Score (Calculated)
Use this if you want the system to calculate the final positions based on raw scores given by multiple judges.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Select a programme and change its judgement type to **Manual Score**.
3. Click **Manage Judges** and add your judges (e.g., Judge A, Judge B, Judge C).
4. Enter the maximum possible score for each judge (e.g., Out of 10 or 20).
5. For each candidate, enter the raw score given by each judge.
6. Click **Save Changes** and then click **Generate Result**.
7. Festie will automatically calculate the totals and assign 1st, 2nd, and 3rd positions!

## Edit a candidate's total

Judges enter scores, and Festie calculates each candidate's total from them. If a total needs a fix, you can type it directly instead of editing judge by judge.

**Who can do this:** admins whose role has the **Edit candidate totals** permission. Roles that could already edit scores get it automatically. Judges, teams, stage volunteers and volunteers can't.

**When it is available:** results are already generated, and the programme uses **Manual Score** or **Online Judgement**. It isn't available for **Manual Result** or round-based programmes.

1. Go to **Programmes** and open the programme.
2. Open the **Scores** tab.
3. Click **Edit totals**. The **Total** column turns into number fields.
4. Type the new total for each candidate you want to change. It must be between 0 and the out-of value.
5. Click **Save totals**.

Festie saves only the totals you changed. If you changed nothing, you see "No totals changed."

### What happens after you save

- Results regenerate. Position, grade and points are recalculated from the new totals.
- If the results were published or checked, they are unpublished. Check and publish them again.
- Edited rows show an **Edited** badge.
- The programme is hidden from the judge panel.
- **Edit score** and **Remove scores** are turned off. You see "Totals edited. Revert to edit judge-wise."

## Revert to judge scores

Use this to go back to the totals calculated from the judges' scores.

1. Open the programme and go to the **Scores** tab.
2. Click **Revert to judge scores**.
3. In the confirmation, click **Revert**.

Festie removes the edited totals and regenerates the results from the judge scores. Judges see the programme again, and you can edit judge by judge again.

<Note>
  If you remove the results, the edited totals are removed too and judge-wise editing is unlocked.
</Note>

## Mark a programme to review

Use this to flag a programme that is scored but needs a second look before you generate its results. Flagged programmes get a **To review** badge, and you can filter the Results page to see only them.

**Who can do this:** admins whose role has the **Mark to review** permission ("Flag evaluated programmes to review."). Roles that can already manage results get it automatically. Judges and teams can't.

**When it is available:** the programme is **Evaluated** (scored, results not generated yet). The button doesn't show for other programmes.

### Mark one programme

1. Go to `Subfest > Results`.
2. Open an **Evaluated** programme.
3. Click **Mark to review**.

The **To review** badge appears next to the status badge on the programme card and at the top of the drawer. The button changes to **Clear review**. Click it to remove the flag.

### Mark several programmes

1. Go to `Subfest > Results` and click **Select**.
2. Tick the programmes.
3. In the bar at the bottom, click **Mark to review** or **Clear review**.

Programmes that are not **Evaluated** are skipped.

### Show only flagged programmes

Click **To review** above the filters. Click it again to turn it off. It works together with the status tabs. It is most useful on the **Scored** tab. The filter is saved in the page link, so you can share the filtered view.

### When the flag clears

- Festie clears it when results are generated for the programme.
- Festie clears it when you remove the scores and the programme goes back to **Draft**.
- Saving scores again does **not** clear it. Click **Clear review** yourself.

<Note>
  The flag belongs to one sub-festival. It is not copied to other sub-festivals when you sync settings.
</Note>

<Note>
  Saving a result does **not** automatically publish it to the public website. You must use the Result Release feature to make it public.
</Note>
