title: "Trophies"
description: "Download trophies for published results and set how they print on the page."

The Trophies page lists programmes with published results. Use it to download trophy print sheets and track which trophies you have downloaded and printed.

## Who can do this

- To open the page, you need access to **Trophies**.
- To download trophies, your role also needs **Download trophies**.
- To mark trophies downloaded or printed, save a page layout and change trophy settings, your role needs permission to change trophies.

## The Trophies page

Open **Results** → **Trophies**. The page has five tabs:

- **Overview**: cards for **Published**, **Trophies**, **To download**, **Partial**, **Downloaded** and **Printed**, plus a progress chart and a chart by category.
- **Published**: programmes with published results that you have not downloaded yet.
- **Partial**: programmes you have downloaded, but some winners did not get a trophy. See [Partially downloaded programmes](#partially-downloaded-programmes).
- **Downloaded**: programmes whose trophies you have downloaded, with nobody missing.
- **Printed**: programmes you have marked as printed.

Each tab except **Overview** shows a count. A programme moves on its own when its trophy file is ready, whether you chose **Single page each** or **Sheet layout**. It moves to **Downloaded**, or to **Partial** if some winners are missing.

At the top right of the page you find the **Trophy settings** button and **Download History**.

## Download trophies

1. Open **Results** → **Trophies**.
2. Open the **Published** tab.
3. Select the programmes you want.
4. In the bar at the bottom, click **Download trophies**.
5. In **Trophy template**, pick a template.
6. Under **Print as**, choose how to print:
   - **Single page each** (the default): one trophy per page, at the template's own size.
   - **Sheet layout**: several trophies on each printed sheet.
7. If you selected a group programme, choose under **Group programmes**:
   - **Each member** (the default): one trophy for every member of a winning group.
   - **One per group**: one trophy for each winning group.

   Festie asks this on every download. It does not save your choice.
8. Click the button:
   - With **Single page each**, click **Download**. Festie starts preparing the file right away.
   - With **Sheet layout**, click **Next**. In **Page layout**, set how trophies sit on each page (see [Set the page layout](#set-the-page-layout)), then click **Download**. To pick a different template, click **Back**.

Festie prepares the file in the background. When it is ready, download it from your background jobs.

<Tip>
  You can also download again from the **Partial**, **Downloaded** and **Printed** tabs.
</Tip>

## Partially downloaded programmes

Your trophy template decides which winners get a trophy page. If its conditions don't cover every winner that **Trophy settings** says should get a trophy, those winners are missing from the file. For example, the template has a condition only for 1st place, but your settings include 1st to 3rd.

When that happens, the programme shows **Partially downloaded**, with the number missing next to it, such as "2 missing". It moves to the **Partial** tab.

To fix it, do one of these:

- **Download the missing trophies.** Select the programmes on the **Partial** tab and click **Download trophies**. Pick a template whose conditions cover the missing winners. The new download adds to what you already have. When nobody is missing, the programme moves to **Downloaded**.
- **Accept it as done.** Select the programmes and click **Mark downloaded**. The programmes move to **Downloaded**. You need permission to change trophies.

If you change **Trophy settings** or the **Group programmes** choice later, Festie uses the new rule from your next download.

## Set the page layout

The **Page layout** step only appears when you choose **Sheet layout**. It opens full screen, with settings on the left and a preview on the right. Click the full-screen button next to the close button to switch back to a smaller window, or to full screen again. The preview shows the template inside each trophy, exactly where and how it will print. Each trophy has a number.

### Page

- **Unit**: the unit for every size on this step: mm, cm, inch, pt or px.
- **Page size**: A3, A4, A5, Letter, Legal or Custom.
- **Page width** and **Page height**: change these to set your own size. The page size switches to **Custom**.
- **Portrait** or **Landscape**: turns the page.

### Trophies

- **Trophy width** and **Trophy height**: the printed size of one trophy.
- **Per page**: how many trophies go on one page, from 1 to 200. Below it, Festie shows how many fit.
- **Margin**: space between the page edge and the trophies.
- **Gap across** and **Gap down**: space between trophies.

Festie places the trophies in a grid, row by row from the top left.

### Mirror trophies

Tick **Mirror** to flip every trophy left to right. Use this when you print on the back of clear material, such as acrylic, so the trophy reads correctly from the front.

Each trophy flips inside its own spot. Positions, sizes and turns on the page stay the same. The preview shows each trophy flipped, and the downloaded file prints them flipped.

### Place trophies yourself

Turn on **Advanced** to place each trophy by hand. A table shows each trophy's **X**, **Y** and **Rotate (°)**. X and Y are measured from the top-left corner of the page.

- Type a value to move or turn a trophy.
- Click **↻** next to a trophy to turn it 90°.

When **Advanced** is on, **Margin**, **Gap across** and **Gap down** are hidden.

### Move and turn trophies on the preview

- **Move**: drag a trophy on the preview. If **Advanced** is off, it turns on.
- **Select**: click a trophy. It gets a dashed outline and a rotate handle above it.
- **Turn**: drag the rotate handle. The trophy turns around its centre in 15° steps. Hold **Shift** to turn it freely.
- **Nudge**: press the arrow keys to move the selected trophy by 1 mm. Hold **Shift** to move it by 10 mm.
- **Clear the selection**: press **Esc**, or click an empty part of the page.

Trophies can overlap.

### Fix layout problems

If something is wrong, Festie shows a message and **Download** stays off until you fix it.

- A trophy outside the page turns red, and its row shows "Outside the page."
- A message like "Only 6 fit on one page." means **Per page** is more than the grid can hold. Lower **Per page**, make the trophies smaller, or reduce the margin and gaps.
- "The item doesn't fit on the page." means one trophy is bigger than the page.
- "Item size is too small." means the trophy width or height is too small.
- "Enter a number in every field." means a field is empty.

### Save the layout

Tick **Save this layout** to use the same layout, including **Mirror**, the next time you download with this template. It is ticked by default. You only see this option if you can change trophies. **Save this layout** applies to **Sheet layout** only.

## Mark trophies printed

1. Open the **Published**, **Partial** or **Downloaded** tab.
2. Select the programmes.
3. In the bar at the bottom, click **Mark printed**.

The programmes move to **Printed**.

To undo:

- On the **Printed** tab, click **Mark not printed** to move programmes back to **Downloaded**.
- On the **Partial**, **Downloaded** or **Printed** tab, click **Mark not downloaded** to move programmes back to **Published**. This clears everything, including the missing count.

You need permission to change trophies for all of these.

## Choose who gets a trophy

1. On the Trophies page, click the **Trophy settings** button at the top right.
2. Under **Who gets a trophy**, choose **Position or grade**, **Position only**, **Grade only** or **Everyone**.
3. If you chose a position option, tick the **Positions** that get a trophy: 1st to 5th.
   **Save** stays off until you tick at least one position.
4. Click **Save**.

The trophy counts and the downloaded trophies follow this setting. You need permission to change trophies to see the settings button.

## See past downloads

1. On the Trophies page, click **Download History** at the top right.
2. Each entry shows the template, when the download finished and how many programmes it included. The newest is first.
3. Click **View programmes** to see the names and codes of the programmes in that download. Click **Back to history** to return.

If a programme no longer exists, it shows as **Programme unavailable**. Deleting an export also removes it from the history.
