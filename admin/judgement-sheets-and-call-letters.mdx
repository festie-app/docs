Festie automatically generates print-ready media for your festival based on the data you enter.

## Download ID cards

ID cards (chest cards) are printed from an ID card template. What each card shows depends on the template you pick. Festie places several cards on each page of one PDF, so you can print and cut them.

### Who can do this

- Admins whose role has the **Download chest card** permission.
- Team managers, for their own team, when the sub-fest allows it. An admin turns this on in **Settings** → **Team Access** → **Download chest cards**.

### Download ID cards for several candidates

1. Open **Candidates**.
2. Select the candidates you want. You can select all.
3. In the bar at the bottom, click **Download Chest Card**.
4. In **ID card template**, pick a template and click **Next**.
5. In **Page layout**, set how the cards sit on each page. See [Set the page layout](#set-the-page-layout).
6. Click **Download**.

Festie prepares the PDF in the background. When it is ready, download it from the downloads panel.

### Download one candidate's ID card

1. Open **Candidates** and click a candidate.
2. At the top of the page, click **Download Chest Card**.
3. Follow steps 4 to 6 above.

### Set the page layout

The **Page layout** step opens full screen, with settings on the left and a preview on the right. Click the full-screen button next to the close button to switch to a smaller window, or back to full screen. The preview shows the template inside each card. Each card has a number.

**Page**

- **Unit**: the unit for every size on this step: mm, cm, inch, pt or px.
- **Page size**: A3, A4, A5, Letter, Legal or Custom.
- **Page width** and **Page height**: change these to set your own size. The page size switches to **Custom**.
- **Portrait** or **Landscape**: turns the page.

**Cards**

- **Card width** and **Card height**: the printed size of one card. These start at the template's real size.
- **Per page**: how many cards go on one page. Below it, Festie shows how many fit.
- **Margin**: space between the page edge and the cards.
- **Gap across** and **Gap down**: space between cards.

Festie places the cards in a grid, row by row from the top left.

**Place cards yourself**

Turn on **Advanced** to place each card by hand. A table shows each card's **X**, **Y** and **Rotate (°)**, measured from the top-left corner of the page. Type a value, or click **↻** to turn a card 90°.

On the preview you can also:

- Drag a card to move it. If **Advanced** is off, it turns on.
- Click a card, then drag the rotate handle above it to turn it. Hold **Shift** to turn it freely.
- Press the arrow keys to nudge the selected card by 1 mm. Hold **Shift** to move it by 10 mm.
- Press **Esc** to clear the selection.

**Fix layout problems**

If something is wrong, Festie shows a message and **Download** stays off until you fix it.

- A card outside the page turns red, and its row shows "Outside the page."
- "Only 6 fit on one page." means **Per page** is more than the grid can hold. Lower **Per page**, make the cards smaller, or reduce the margin and gaps.
- "The item doesn't fit on the page." means one card is bigger than the page.
- "Item size is too small." means the card width or height is too small.
- "Enter a number in every field." means a field is empty.

**Save the layout**

**Save this layout** is ticked by default. It keeps this layout on the template, so it is filled in the next time anyone downloads with it. Only admins with the **Download chest card** permission see this option. Team managers can set a layout, but it isn't saved.

## Downloading Judgement Sheets & Participant Lists

Before a programme begins, you can print official sheets for your judges and organizers.

1. Navigate to `Subfest > Programmes`.
2. Select a programme and click **Download**.
3. You will see several options:
   - **Participant List:** A list of everyone participating (Group lists are formatted differently than Individual lists).
   - **Judgement Sheet:** A blank scoring sheet pre-filled with candidate chest numbers.
   - **Certificates:** Blank certificates of merit or participation.
   - **Posters:** Promotional posters for the event (e.g., "First Grade", "With Grade", etc.).
4. Select what you need, and the PDF will be generated instantly.
