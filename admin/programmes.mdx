Programmes represent the actual competitions happening in your festival (e.g., Essay Writing, 100m Dash). Because every competition has unique rules, Festie provides extensive settings to configure them.

## Prerequisites

- Categories and Teams are set up.
- You know the specific rules for the competition (limits, scoring, team participation).

Navigate to `Subfest > Programmes` to view, add, or import your programmes.

---

## Adding a Programme Manually

Click the **Add Programme** button. You will go through several configuration tabs:

### 1. Basic Details
- **Programme Code:** A unique identifier for the programme. For example, a Junior Song might be `JN-01` or `F01`.
- **Programme Name:** The official name of the item.
- **Programme Type:** Choose **Individual** (candidates participate alone) or **Group** (candidates participate as a team of 3, 4, etc.).
- **Judgement Type:** How will the results be recorded?
  - *Manual Result:* Admins directly input who came 1st, 2nd, 3rd.
  - *Manual Score:* Admins input the raw scores given by the judges on paper, and the system calculates the result.
  - *Online Judgement:* Judges use a separate online portal to enter scores directly on their phones.
- **Category:** The category this programme belongs to.
- **Collections & Skills:** Group the programme into Collections (like "Stage" or "Off-stage") and tag it with Skills (like "Language" or "Athletics") for deep analytics later.
- **Concept Note:** A brief explanation of what the programme is about.

### 2. Registration Settings
- **Open Registration:** Choose if registration is open immediately.
- **Topic Registration:** Enable this if candidates must register a specific topic (e.g., Speech Topic) prior to the event.
- **Substitution Rules:** Define if team managers are allowed to swap candidates later due to medical or other reasons.

### 3. Participant Settings (Curbs & Limits)
- **Min/Max Limits:** Set the minimum and maximum number of candidates/groups a team can field for this programme. For no limit, leave them as `0`.
- **Team Eligibility:** By default, "All Teams can participate" is checked. If this is a restricted event, uncheck it and specify which teams are allowed.
- **Category Eligibility:** By default, only candidates from the selected Category can participate. For "General" events where anyone can join, uncheck this box and specify the allowed categories.

### 4. Score Settings
- Enable or disable **Position Criteria** and **Grade Criteria** based on whether this event awards them.
- **Point Percentages:** You can control how many points are actually awarded. If a 1st place gives 10 points, but you want the candidate to receive 0 points (perhaps it's a fun event), you can set the *Candidate Point Percentage* to `0%`. You can adjust this for both Candidate and Team percentages independently.

### 5. Call Letter Settings
- **Generation Mode:** Choose whether the system should generate call letters using Alphabets (A, B, C) or Numbers (1, 2, 3).
- **Custom Order:** For certain sports or track events, you may need teams to perform in a specific order (e.g., Alpha Team first, Blue Team second). You can manually set the team appearance order here.

Click **Save** once you've configured the settings. The programme will appear in your list.

## Judgment settings

Judgment settings control how judges score a programme and how ties are handled. Open a programme and go to **Settings** > **Judgment**.

You need permission to edit programme settings to change them.

- **Judgement criteria:** The scoring columns judges use.
- **Judgement window:** When judges can score. **Open** means always, **Closed** means never, and **Scheduled** means between the start and end time you set.
- **Judgment settings:** Choose **Sub-fest default** to use the sub-fest's settings, or **Custom** to set this programme's own.
- **Show concept and instructions:** Shows the instruction you type here on the judge's scorecard.
- **Show timer for performance duration:** Gives judges a timer.
- **Require judge comment:** Judges must write a comment for each candidate.
- **Lock on submission:** Judges can't edit scores after they submit.
- **Require selfie before judgment:** Judges need a selfie on file before they can submit scores.
- **Allow generated ties:** Equal scores share the same rank and position.
- **Tie breaker mode:** Used when ties are not allowed. Choose **Lead judge**, **Judgment column** or **None**.
- **Tie breaker column:** The criteria column that breaks ties, when the mode is **Judgment column**.
- **Candidate details visible to judges:** Choose what judges see for each candidate, such as code letter, chest number, name or team.

<Note>
Only programmes with **Online Judgement** show all of these settings. Other programmes show only **Judgement criteria** and **Judgement window**. If you renamed terms like "judge" or "candidate", the labels use your names.
</Note>

### Change judgment settings after results

Once a programme has results (generated, checked or published), most judgment settings stay editable. Changing them does not affect scores or results.

These settings are locked while results exist:

- **Judgement criteria**
- **Allow generated ties** (shown as **Allow ties in generated results** on the results page)
- **Tie breaker mode** (shown as **Tie breaker**)
- **Tie breaker column** (shown as **Tie-breaking criteria column**)

You can still change everything else, including the judgement window, instructions, timer, judge comment, lock on submission, selfie and visible candidate details.

**From the programme settings:**

1. Open the programme and go to **Settings** > **Judgment**.
2. You see the note "Criteria and tie settings are locked while results exist." Only the locked rows are greyed out.
3. Change the settings you need and save.

**From the results page:**

1. Open the programme's results and go to the settings tab.
2. You see the note "Tie settings are locked while results exist."
3. Change the other settings and click **Save settings**.

To change tie settings, remove the generated results first. To change the criteria, remove the generated results and the judges' scores, because scores are saved per criteria column. This is about switching a programme to a different criteria. To change the max marks of the criteria itself after scoring, see [Judgement criteria](/admin/judgement-criteria#change-max-marks-after-scoring). If you switch **Judgment settings** between **Sub-fest default** and **Custom**, and that would change the tie settings, you see "Remove generated results before changing tie settings for this programme."

### Change judgment settings for many programmes

1. Go to **Programmes** and select the programmes you want to change.
2. Click **Settings**. The **Bulk Programme Settings** dialog opens.
3. Under **Judgment Settings**, tick the rows you want to change and set their values.
4. Click **Review Changes**. Check the list under **Settings to apply** and **Programmes to update**. Remove anything you don't want with the **X**.
5. Click **Apply Settings**.

Judgment settings also apply to programmes that already have results. The exceptions are tie settings and judgement criteria: if a selected programme has results, changes to those are refused.
